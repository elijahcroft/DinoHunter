    A walkable reconstruction · 66 million years ago

    Hell Creek, the last summer

    You are standing on a humid coastal floodplain in what is now eastern Montana, a few thousand years before the Chicxulub impact. Everything alive here lived here together — no Stegosaurus, no Velociraptor, no animals borrowed from other continents or other tens of millions of years. Eight species, built to current evidence: horizontal spines, lipped teeth, feathers where feathers are known, and no pronated “bunny hands” anywhere.

    Underfoot and overhead: Metasequoia dawn redwoods, Sabalites palms, tree ferns, ginkgo, and the early flowering shrubs that were already taking over the understory.
