    You have one bull, and sixteen rounds

    A humid coastal floodplain in what is now eastern Montana. Somewhere out along the channel is a mature bull Triceratops prorsus — nine tonnes, and the most common large animal in this formation. He is licensed to you. Nothing else here is.

    He will smell you long before he sees you. Watch the wind, keep it in your face, and stay low. A bull that is merely alert can be settled by standing still. A bull that has your scent is gone.

    Put the first round through the lungs, behind the shoulder. A gut shot will not stop him — it will just make you walk a very long way.
